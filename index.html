Three stages.
No surprises.

You'll know where the job is at every day, from the first site visit to the final walkthrough.

STAGE 01

Site visit and scope

Roman walks the site with you, listens to what you want, and flags what the building will and won't allow. You get a written estimate within days.

Free, no obligation
