## 6. Homeowner FAQ, in Roman's voice

**How long will it take?** I lock the timeline before we start, after the permit and materials are confirmed. If something changes, you hear it from me the same day.
**How much deposit?** Enough to order materials, never the bulk of the job. The rest is tied to milestones you can see.
**Are you insured?** Yes, licensed and insured. Ask any contractor for proof before they step on site. I'll show you mine.
**Who is on my site?** Subcontractors I have used for decades, and me. Daily.
**Do you do small jobs?** I do renovations, custom homes and commercial fit-outs. If your project is smaller, call anyway and I'll point you the right way.
**Where do you work?** Hamilton, London, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and across the GTA.
**What does the estimate include?** Scope line by line, materials, labour, permits, timeline. If it is not on paper, it is not in the price.
